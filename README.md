## Read before you compare

- **AdMakeAI**: A standard (Fast) image ad costs 2 credits; higher quality costs 8 or 20. UGC video clips cost 5, 15 or 50 credits.
- **AdCreative.ai**: Pricing is on the homepage (/pricing returns 404). The yearly prices are a Black Friday offer on the page (50% off quarterly and yearly plans). A credit is used per downloaded ad.
- **AdStellar**: About 100 credits per AI image ad, so roughly 500 image ads a month. Monthly billing only.
- **Creatify**: Videos per credit depend on the model, so there is no per-video count.
- **HeyGen**: Business adds $20 per seat per month for each team member.
- **MakeUGC**: Pro's credits are a 2,000 to 5,000 range set by a slider. The trial costs $1.
- **Omneky**: An HD image ad costs 7 credits (14 at 4K).
- **Pencil**: A generation is any AI call, not one finished ad.
- **Predis.ai**: The yearly prices are a BFCM offer on the page (up to 50% off yearly plans). The FAQ prices an image ad creative at 15 credits; the plan cards say about 325 images for 1,300 credits.
- **Zeely**: Shown as discounted from $49.95, $89.95 and $149.95 and marked "Available only on the site". No yearly price or free tier shown.

Not listed, because their pricing pages showed no price on November 10, 2026:

- **Arcads** (https://www.arcads.ai): arcads.ai/pricing returns 404 and the homepage shows no prices.
- **Madgicx** (https://madgicx.com/pricing): The pricing page shows no fixed price; the plan price is a placeholder filled in by a script.

## Method

- Prices are in USD before tax, as each vendor's pricing page showed them on the date checked. No estimates.
- "Billed monthly" is the price on monthly billing; "Yearly, per month" is the monthly equivalent on yearly billing.
- Enterprise and contact-sales plans are left out.
- "Per unit" is the monthly price divided by the image ads or videos a plan makes, only where the page states that number.
- Vendors change prices often and run temporary offers. Check the source link before relying on a number, and open an issue if one is out of date.
